# Miniatura · prompt para ChatGPT (copiar entero)

Consejo: sube a ChatGPT una captura oficial como base (por ejemplo `Port_Gellhorn_01` o `Ambrosia_02` de la galería de Rockstar) y pega esto:

```
Create a YouTube thumbnail, 1280x720 (16:9), ultra high contrast, bold and simple, readable on a phone in one second.

Scene: a night-time swamp road in a tropical Florida-like state, palm trees in silhouette, a beam of white-green light coming down from a glowing UFO in the top right corner. In the foreground on the left, a big, wet, hairy swamp creature (a "skunk ape" / Bigfoot) peeking out from behind a mangrove tree, looking straight at the viewer, slightly menacing but fun. In the bottom right corner, a small cartoon-realistic dog wearing goggles on a jet ski, for a touch of humor.

Text: only 2 huge words on the left side, stacked: "18 SECRETS" in thick condensed white letters with a hot pink (#FF2E8C) outline and a soft purple glow. Under it, a small yellow (#FFCE28) tag that says "GTA 6".

Colors: deep black-blue night (#080810), neon purple (#8B35FF) and hot pink (#FF2E8C) rim lighting, yellow accent (#FFCE28). Cinematic, realistic game-art style, sharp focus, strong depth.

Rules: no real logos, no official game logo, no brand names, no small text, no watermarks. Leave the bottom-right 15% free of important details (YouTube shows the video length there).
```

Notas:
- No pedir el logo de GTA VI ni personajes oficiales (Jason, Lucia): ChatGPT suele negarse y además no lo usamos.
- Promete lo que el vídeo cumple: el ovni, el Skunk Ape y Cool Dog salen en el vídeo.
- Alternativa B (si quieres una más limpia): texto "THEY HID THIS" + huevo de colores en lo alto de una torre de radio al atardecer.
