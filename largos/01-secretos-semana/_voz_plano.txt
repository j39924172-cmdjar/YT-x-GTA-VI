Players have been inside GTA 6 for just a few days. And they have already found a literal Easter egg on top of a tower, a dog that saves you on a jet ski, and a man who knows he is living inside a video game. These are eighteen secrets players have found so far, from the first week of GTA 6 and even from before it came out. Rockstar announced none of these. Players found them by climbing, swimming, and even digging through game files. Stay until the end, because number eighteen is out of this world.  Part one. Hidden in plain sight. These are the secrets that were right in front of everyone, if you knew where to press or where to climb.  Number one. The money cheat. Yes, GTA 6 has cheat codes again. Press up, right, down, left, up, left, down, right, and two hundred and fifty thousand dollars land in your account. But there is a catch. This code only unlocks after you finish the story. So no, you cannot buy a supercar in the first hour. Other cheats are earned by playing, which is a very Rockstar way of saying: you have to work for your crimes. One warning, though. Some websites are mixing up GTA 5 cheats with GTA 6 ones. If a code does nothing, that is probably why.  Number two. The first person mode that does not exist. Rockstar did not include an official first person mode at launch. Players did not care. One player found a way to get it anyway, by remapping the camera button and using the phone inside a vehicle. And the strangest part? The camera even moves with the character's head as he walks. Officially, it is not there. Unofficially, people are already playing with it. Would you play the whole story like that? Let me know, because I honestly cannot decide.  Number three. An actual Easter egg. Somewhere in Leonida there is a giant tower, and climbing it takes around six to seven minutes. Most players would give up halfway. The ones who did not found a bird's nest at the very top, full of painted eggs. A real Easter egg. And if you throw one off the tower, a short cutscene plays, and the egg smashes into a car windscreen far below. Long time fans noticed something else. GTA Vice City, back in 2002, also hid a literal Easter egg. Rockstar just did it again, twenty four years later. Same joke, a whole new generation of players, and a much longer climb.  Number four. The way down. Climbing for seven minutes is one thing. Climbing back down is another. But reports say there is a parachute waiting at the top of that same tower. So you get the egg, you throw it, and then you base jump off the tower like nothing happened. That is the GTA way to leave a building. And it means you can grab a parachute very early, before the story even gives you one.  Part two. Movie and TV tributes. Rockstar loves pop culture, and this time the references are hiding in garages, trunks and apartment blocks.  Number five. Paul Walker's car. In a villa garage on Vespucci Island, in Vice City, players found a green Maibatsu Penumbra F F. If that looks familiar, it should. It is a tribute to the green nineteen ninety five Mitsubishi Eclipse that Brian O'Conner drove in the first Fast and Furious, back in 2001. Paul Walker played Brian, and this is Rockstar's quiet tribute to him. Even better, you can take it very early in the game, and it is yours to keep. But before you drive off, there is one more thing you should know about that car.  Number six. The snake in the trunk. Rockstar did not stop there. If you open the trunk of that car, a snake jumps out and attacks you. Players instantly recognized the joke. There is a snake in my boot. Yes, that is the Toy Story line, and yes, Rockstar hid it in the trunk of a Fast and Furious tribute. Two movie references in one car. And here is a fun coincidence. Toy Story came out in 1995, the same model year as that Eclipse. Rockstar's artists clearly had a lot of fun with this garage.  Number seven. Dexter's apartment. Fans of the show Dexter spotted a building on Washington Island, in Vice City, that looks almost identical to Dexter Morgan's waterfront apartment block. Rockstar has not confirmed it as a tribute. But the community is convinced, and honestly, it is hard to argue with them after number eight.  Number eight. The kill kit and the boat. Players who explored the area reported finding a collection of tools that look a lot like Dexter's signature kit. And nearby, there is a boat that looks just like his boat from the show, the Slice of Life. Players say you can even steal it. Rockstar has said nothing. Which, for Rockstar, is basically a wink. Dexter was set in Miami, and Vice City is Rockstar's Miami. So honestly, it would have been stranger if they had left him out.  Before we keep going, if you are enjoying this, subscribe. It is free, and it tells me to keep making these. Part three. Rockstar's own history. GTA 6 is full of little winks to the games that came before it.  Number nine. Michael's boat. This one started before launch. In the second trailer, fans spotted a white boat with blue stripes parked outside Jason's house. It looks like the same model Michael owned in GTA 5. And here is the twist. Jason's house belongs to Brian Heder, a man who also owns a marina. A marina owner with a stolen looking boat in his yard? Fans connected the dots very quickly. Remember that mission where Michael's boat gets stolen? Fans think it may have ended up right here, in Leonida. Even Michael's voice actor, Ned Luke, joked online that he was coming for his boat. Some fans point out small differences, so this one stays a theory. But it is a fun one. And if it is true, it means the worlds of GTA 5 and GTA 6 are connected by one stolen boat.  Number ten. Red Dead money. Look closely at the cash in the second trailer. The faces on the bills are President Hardin and President Thaddeus Waxman, from the Red Dead Redemption series. Those portraits come from Red Dead Redemption 2's collectible cigarette cards. So the money in Leonida quietly suggests that Arthur Morgan's world and Jason and Lucia's world are the same universe, just a century apart. Rockstar has never confirmed it. But it never had to.  Number eleven. A boat called Leonida. This one was hiding for years. In January, a dataminer found a partly unused boat texture inside Red Dead Redemption 2's files. It lists several ship names. And one of them is The Leonida. Red Dead 2 came out in 2018, long before anyone knew the name of GTA 6's state. Was it a planned tease? Some say yes, others say it is a coincidence. Either way, it is a wild find.  Number twelve. The Vercetti Estate. This is not a fan theory. It comes from Rockstar itself. The Ultimate Edition includes a pair of revolvers, and Rockstar's own description says their style was sourced from the Vercetti Estate, with palm tree grips. The Vercetti Estate was Tommy Vercetti's mansion in GTA Vice City. It was where he ran his empire, and where the game's final shootout took place. Tommy is not in GTA 6. But his house, it seems, never left. For anyone who played the original Vice City, that single line in a product description says a lot.  Number thirteen. Games inside the game. Your safehouse is not just a place to sleep. According to Kotaku, you can play real video games on an in-game console, including first person and top down shooter mini games. Rockstar built games, inside a game, inside a game console that parodies the one you are probably holding right now. That is three layers deep. And it is a perfect excuse to stay inside on a rainy day in Leonida.  Part four. Leonida's wild side. Leonida is Rockstar's version of Florida, and Florida is famous for being a little wild.  Number fourteen. The fighter jet. In GTA 5, stealing a jet meant breaking into Fort Zancudo. GTA 6 has its own version. At Mile Zero Air Station, on Goose Key, there is a fighter jet sitting on the base. And you can reach it surprisingly early in the game. Of course, there is a price. Take it, and you instantly get five stars, plus two other fighter jets chasing you through the sky. It is one of the fastest ways to go from nobody to public enemy number one. Good luck landing it in one piece.  Number fifteen. Cool Dog. Swim too far from the shore, and you might hear barking. Then a dog in goggles and a life jacket arrives on a jet ski to rescue you. Players named him Cool Dog. If you praise him instead of scolding him, he lets you ride along to his own island. Reports say you can find around twelve hundred dollars there. A dog. On a jet ski. Giving you money. Only in Leonida. If there is one secret in this video you should try tonight, it is this one.  Number sixteen. Skunk Ape. Florida has its own Bigfoot legend, called the Skunk Ape. People in the Everglades have claimed to see it for decades. And Rockstar put it in the game. In the swamps south of Cottonmouth Lake, below Hamlet, players have caught a huge hairy creature eating a deer on a dirt road. It is not a random sighting either. It leads into a strange side quest. Bigfoot fans have waited years for this, and Rockstar finally delivered. And this is the moment the video gets weird, because the last two secrets go even further.  Part five. The truth is out there. We saved the strangest for last.  Number seventeen. The man who knows he is in a video game. This story started in twenty twenty one. GTA San Andreas Definitive Edition had a strange image of a modern house that did not belong in that game. Fans wondered if it was a first look at GTA 6. On launch day, a player called Jared, from the GTA mapping community, found that same house in Belville, in Leonard County. And inside the garage, there is a hideout. It belongs to a conspiracy theorist who has figured out he lives inside a video game. His notes list the clues. People who look identical. Neighbors who cannot get into their own homes. People glitching through walls. Buildings with nothing behind their locked doors. Nobody knows if Rockstar planned this back in twenty twenty one. It is also possible they reused that house later, when they built Belville. But it is one of the smartest jokes in the whole game.  Number eighteen. The U F O. Rockstar has hidden aliens in GTA for years, and GTA 6 keeps the tradition. Head to Kelly County, just outside Port Gellhorn, at night. Guides say around two in the morning is the time to try. You will need chapter two of the story unlocked first. Then look for a car on the side of the road with its lights flashing. It does not appear every time, so be patient. Follow the strange light into the woods, and you find a woman being abducted. Help her, and walk back to the car. That is when the U F O comes back, and takes you instead. Your reward is a hidden trophy, called The Truth Is Out There. GTA 5 had its Mount Chiliad mystery, which took fans more than a decade to work out. GTA 6 just started its own. And something tells me this U F O is only the first clue.  That is eighteen secrets, and most of them were found in a single week. And this is just the beginning. Rockstar has not said how many secrets are hidden in Leonida, and players are still digging. My favourites? The literal Easter egg, Cool Dog, and the man who knows he lives in a video game.
