Yellow or red? Before you steal a car in GTA 6, check this one color. It decides if you get to keep it. Fact one: scan the car with the Waink app and you'll see its tracker. A yellow tracker? Good news. Take it to a Pay and Spray, register it, and it's yours. Fact two: here's the catch. A red tracker pings the car's location while you drive. And early on, you can't register it, sell it, or store it. You're basically driving a GPS beacon. Fact three: the Key Cloner won't save you. You get it in chapter three, and it opens the lock, but it doesn't touch the tracker. Some guides say you can remove trackers later in the story. So before you steal a car in GTA 6, check that one color. Follow for more GTA 6 secrets.
