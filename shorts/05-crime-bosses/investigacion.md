# 05 · GTA 6 Crime Bosses — investigación (Jarvis + Nobita)

Encargo de la oficina: **"Every GTA 6 Gang Leader in 30 Seconds"** (encargo 7qvsc4zmt8ohspfhn0b8), 18-11-2026.

## Cambio de título y por qué
- Rockstar **no llama "gang leader"** a ningún personaje y no existe una lista oficial de "todos" los líderes de bandas. "Every" no se puede verificar.
- Lo que sí está en la fuente primaria: tres personajes que **mandan en su negocio criminal** (un imperio, una banda de atracadores, un negocio de contrabando).
- Título nuevo: **"3 GTA 6 Crime Bosses in 30 Seconds"** (no promete "todos").
- Dre'Quan y las Real Dimez quedan fuera: Rockstar dice que Dre'Quan es "more of a hustler than a gangster".

## Datos verificados (fuente primaria: https://www.rockstargames.com/VI/only-in-leonida, consultada el 18-11-2026)
1. **Boobie Ike** — "Boobie is a local Vice City legend — and acts like it. One of the few to transform his time in the streets into a legitimate empire spanning real estate, a strip club, and a recording studio — Boobie's all smiles until it's time to talk business."
2. **Raul Bautista** — "Confidence, charm, and cunning — Raul's a seasoned bank robber always on the hunt for talent ready to take the risks that bring the biggest rewards." / "Raul's recklessness raises the stakes with every score. Sooner or later, his crew will have to double down or pull their chips from the table."
3. **Brian Heder** — "Brian's a classic drug runner from the golden age of smuggling in the Keys. Still moving product through his boat yard with his third wife, Lori, Brian's been around long enough to let others do his dirty work."
