Paul Walker's car is hiding in GTA 6. And yes, you can drive it. Fact one: it's a green Maibatsu Penumbra. With the same blue graphics as the Mitsubishi Eclipse that Brian drove in the very first Fast and Furious. A real tribute to Paul Walker. Fact two: you'll find it in a villa garage on Vespucci Island, in Vice City. No mission, no money. Just drive over, slip into the garage and take it. Want to keep it? Once registering unlocks in chapter two, take it to a Pay and Spray. Fact three: here's the crazy part. Open the trunk, and a snake attacks you. Yep, that's a Toy Story joke. There's a snake in my boot, right? So go find Paul Walker's car before anyone else does. Follow for more GTA 6 secrets.
