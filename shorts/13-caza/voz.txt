There's a fighter jet waiting for you in GTA 6. And you can just take it. Fact one: it's parked at Mile Zero Air Station, a military base on Goose Key, in the southwest of Leonida. Fact two: you don't have to wait. The base opens up early, after your first missions, so ram the gate or sneak in by boat. Fact three: when you take off, you get a five-star wanted level, and the military comes after you hard. So save first, then go grab your fighter jet. Follow for more GTA 6 secrets.
