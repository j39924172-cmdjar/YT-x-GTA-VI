## Datos verificados (2 medios o más)
1. **L3 + R3 (PS5) o LS + RS (Xbox), pulsados a la vez, activan el modo Focus.**
   - GameSpot "GTA 6 Tips: Everything I Wish I Knew Before I Started Playing" — https://www.gamespot.com/articles/gta-6-tips-everything-i-wish-i-knew-before-i-started-playing/
   - Yahoo Tech / The Big Lead "8 GTA 6 tips…": "press both analogue sticks in at once to enter Focus Mode" — https://tech.yahoo.com/gaming/articles/8-gta-6-tips-game-115452979.html
2. **En Focus, los enemigos salen en rojo, los objetos con los que interactuar en amarillo y el dinero en verde.**
   - Red Bull "GTA 6 beginner's guide: Essential tips and hidden mechanics" — https://www.redbull.com/in-en/gta-6-beginners-guide
   - TheClick "GTA 6 Focus Ability: Controls, Weak Points and Upgrades" — https://www.theclick.gg/gta-6-focus-ability/
3. **Un efecto de sonido circular avisa de que cerca hay un encuentro único.**
   - Yahoo Tech / The Big Lead: "you can see a circular audio effect whenever there's something nearby that will lead to a unique encounter"
   - GameSpot (mismo artículo de consejos)
