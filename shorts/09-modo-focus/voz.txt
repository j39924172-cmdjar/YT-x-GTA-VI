Press both sticks in GTA 6. The game never really tells you why, and most players never try it. Trick one: click L3 and R3 on PlayStation, or both sticks on Xbox, and you switch into Focus mode. The world slows down and the colors change. Trick two: by default, enemies glow red, things you can use turn yellow, and cash shows up in green. So you can spot a wallet on the floor, or an enemy hiding behind a car, before anyone sees you. Trick three is the crazy part. When you hear a circular sound pulse, stop. It means something unique is nearby, an encounter you could easily walk right past. Most people do. You won't. So next time you explore Leonida, do one thing first. Press both sticks.
